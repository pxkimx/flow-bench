Flow Bench: how to open it
==========================

1. Drag "Flow Bench.app" into your Applications folder (or anywhere you like).

2. Double-click it. The app is free and not registered with Apple, so the first time macOS may say it
   cannot be opened. Click Done, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
   "Open Anyway". (On macOS 14 or older: Control-click the app and choose Open.)
   Or, in Terminal: type  xattr -cr  and a space, drag the app into the window, press Return.

3. The first start sets up its own Python environment in
   ~/Library/Application Support/Flow Bench   (a few minutes, needs internet once).
   It needs Python 3.10 to 3.14; Python 3.12 from python.org is the safe choice.

4. Flow Bench opens in your web browser at http://localhost:8800. That tab is the app's window.
   Closing the tab quits Flow Bench completely.

Your FCS files are read where they are and never changed. Nothing is uploaded. The two example datasets
are downloaded from Zenodo only if you ask for them (Home > Examples).

If it will not start, the log is at ~/Library/Logs/FlowBench.log
